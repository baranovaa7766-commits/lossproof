Compare Cash-Out Services

A cash-out service (also called an "off-ramp" in industry jargon) converts USDT or other crypto into rubles or another regular currency and sends it to your card. This is the second, usually the more expensive, stage of a withdrawal — compare the fee and terms before you pick one.

"Vetted" here means: the service has a publicly checkable license, or it plainly publishes its own rates on its official site. That's not a safety guarantee or financial advice — rates and fees change without notice, and cash-out services don't have a public API for checking rates live.

How we pick these services

Only services with either a publicly checkable license, or openly published rates on their own official site (not a rough estimate, an actual page with numbers) make this comparison — no anonymous exchangers without an official site and with an opaque rate.

A7A5 is deliberately not included here. It's not a standalone cash-out service but a ruble-pegged stablecoin from A7/Old Vector LLC, traded mainly on the Grinex exchange — and Grinex and A7A5-linked structures were placed under US Treasury OFAC sanctions in August 2025 as the successor to the sanctioned Garantex exchange.

There's also BestChange, an exchanger aggregator you can cross-check current rates against. It's a reference resource, not an affiliate service.

Once you've picked a service, run the total amount you'd receive across both stages (exchange + cash-out) through the transfer calculator.
