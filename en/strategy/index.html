Trading Strategy Finder

12 questions about your goals, experience and attitude to risk — and you'll see which crypto strategies fit your profile, and which exchanges have the tools for them. About three minutes; your answers stay in your browser.

How the finder works

  • Safety first, then scoring. Leveraged and futures strategies are only offered with trading experience, consent to leverage, a calm reaction to drawdowns, and when it isn't most of your savings. No combination of other answers overrides this.
  • Then matching. The remaining strategies score points for matching your goal, time, horizon, decision style, analysis approach and starting amount. We show the best match and up to two close ones.
  • Exclusions are visible. Below the result is the list of strategies we didn't suggest, with a reason for each.
  • Exchanges without partnerships. Exchanges are ordered only by whether they have the tools the strategy needs and what they cost. We have no partnerships with exchanges; links go to our own exchange overviews.
  • No forecasts or promises. We don't name coins, forecast prices or show strategy returns: past results guarantee nothing.
  • Your answers go nowhere. Matching runs in your browser: we don't store your answers or send them anywhere.

We deliberately left out P2P arbitrage (risk of criminal liability under Russia's law on "droppers" and account freezes under anti-money-laundering rules), trading new listings and memecoins (effectively gambling, with a high share of scams), as well as options and spot margin trading.

Sources

  • Day-trading statistics: Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti, "Day Trading for a Living?" — a study of the Brazilian futures market: of those who day traded for more than 300 days, 97% lost money.
  • Regular purchases vs. investing at once: Vanguard's study "Dollar-cost averaging just means taking risk later" — in stock markets, investing at once more often did better, about two times out of three.
  • Exchange tools (demo account, bots, copy trading, Earn, Proof of Reserves, futures fees) — exchanges' official pages and help centers, some features from reviews. Details per exchange are in Compare exchanges.
