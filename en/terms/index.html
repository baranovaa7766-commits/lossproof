Glossary

The site uses a lot of trading and crypto jargon that isn't obvious to everyone. Here's what it all means, in plain language.

Prop firms & trading

Prop firm (proprietary trading firm)
A company that gives a trader its own money to trade, in exchange for a share of the profit. You don't put up your own capital — just pay for the evaluation.
Evaluation / challenge
A paid test of profitable trading under the firm's rules. Pass it, and you get an account funded with the firm's money.
Funded account
The account a trader gets after passing the evaluation. From here on, you're trading the firm's money, not your own.
Profit split
The share of profit that goes to the trader. "80/20" means the trader keeps 80%, the firm keeps 20%.
Drawdown
How far the account balance is allowed to drop before the firm closes it. Daily drawdown covers one day; total (or max) drawdown covers the whole account's history.
Trailing drawdown
A drawdown limit that recalculates from the account's highest-ever balance, not from the starting balance — so the loss limit "trails" upward as you profit.
Scaling
Gradually increasing the account size as a trader keeps trading profitably.
Broker-backing
A licensed, regulated broker actually stands behind the prop firm. Not a guarantee of trustworthiness, but an extra point in its favor.

Exchanges & crypto

USDT / USDC (stablecoin)
A cryptocurrency whose price is pegged to roughly 1 US dollar. Used as a "digital dollar" for transfers, not for trading gains.
Spot
A regular buy or sell of crypto at the current price, with no borrowed funds or complex instruments involved.
Taker / maker fee
Taker: your order fills instantly at the market price (fee is usually higher). Maker: your order sits in the order book until someone else fills it (fee is usually lower).
P2P
A direct deal between two people through an exchange acting as a middleman: one sells crypto for rubles, the other buys it, and the money goes straight to a card.
KYC (Know Your Customer)
The mandatory identity check when signing up on an exchange: a passport, sometimes a selfie holding it.
VASP (Virtual Asset Service Provider)
An official registration or license for companies that work with crypto, granted by a specific country's regulator.
MiCA / CASP
The EU's crypto-market regulation law (MiCA) and the type of license it grants to crypto companies (CASP).
TRC20 / ERC20 network
The technical rail USDT moves over. TRC20 (Tron network) is almost always much cheaper in fees than ERC20 (Ethereum network).

Withdrawals & transfers

Cash-out service (off-ramp)
A service that converts crypto into rubles or another regular currency and sends it to your card.
Spread
The gap between the rate a service offers and the "fair" mid-market rate. The smaller the spread, the better the deal.
Mid-market rate
The exchange rate with no markup added — what you typically see in the news, not what a bank or cash-out service will actually give you.
Affiliate link
A link that may earn the site a commission if you sign up through it. It doesn't affect the rate or fee you get.
Sanctions list
Companies and individuals that governments (the US, the EU, etc.) have banned others from doing business with. It's why we exclude certain services from our comparisons — details are always noted alongside.
