How to Withdraw From Topstep

Topstep payout methods, fees, and minimums — plus a transfer calculator to see the cheapest way to turn your payout into your local currency.

Topstep payout methods

MethodsProp-to-Brokerage and Aeropay (US only), Wise (international), ACH (US), Wire/SWIFT (international)
Firm-side feeWise, Prop-to-Brokerage, and Aeropay carry no fee; ACH and Wire cost $30
Minimum withdrawal$125
Typical speedApproval takes 1-3 business days; delivery is same-day (Prop-to-Brokerage/Aeropay), 1-3 days (ACH/Wise), or 5-10 days (Wire)
Prop-to-Brokerage and Aeropay are only available to US-based traders. For traders in Russia and the CIS, the real choice is between Wise (no fee from Topstep, though Wise itself doesn't work for Russian residents either — see the crypto payout guide) and Wire/SWIFT ($30 fee).
These figures were collected via research (September 2026) and may be out of date. Always confirm current fees and limits on your Topstep dashboard before withdrawing.
