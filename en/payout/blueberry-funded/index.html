Blueberry Funded payout methods

MethodsUSDC / USDT-TRC20 (up to $2,000 per request), RiseWorks (for larger amounts)
Firm-side feeNone — but RiseWorks typically runs ~10% on larger amounts, and crypto has a network fee
Minimum withdrawal$100
Typical speed1-2 business days processing; payouts every 14 days (faster with paid upgrades: 7-day / 3-day / on-demand)
Crypto payouts (USDC/USDT-TRC20) are capped at $2,000 per request — larger amounts route through RiseWorks automatically, where the fee is noticeably higher.
These figures were collected via research (September 2026) and may be out of date. Always confirm current fees and limits on your Blueberry Funded dashboard before withdrawing.
