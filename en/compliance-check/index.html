Prop Firm Compliance Check

Enter your current balance, equity, and trading days completed — see whether your daily or overall drawdown limit is breached, and whether you've hit the profit target, against a specific firm's exact rules. Free, no sign-up, nothing saved.

How it works, and why only 6 firms for now

Rules come from the same data as the prop firm comparison — drawdown percentages, calculation type (static or trailing), and phase-by-phase profit targets. Topstep isn't supported yet: its limits are fixed dollar amounts per account size rather than percentages — that's a different calculation model we'll add separately rather than force into the same logic inaccurately.
