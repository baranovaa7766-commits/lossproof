Affiliate Disclosure

This site may earn a commission when you sign up for a service through links on this page. This does not affect the rates or fees you pay, and does not influence our comparisons. We recommend independently verifying all fees and terms directly with the provider before making a decision.

How we choose what to show

The calculator on this site ranks payout methods purely by the estimated amount you'd receive, based on publicly available fee and markup information. Whether or not we have an affiliate relationship with a provider has no bearing on that ranking or on the numbers shown.

Where our data comes from

Prop-firm payout terms are collected manually from each firm's own documentation and support resources, and are reviewed periodically — they can still be out of date. Exchange rates come from the free ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) — unlike ECB-based rates, it publishes the ruble and other CIS currencies. Spread and fee figures for the crypto exchanges (Bybit, Bitget, KuCoin) are estimates based on typical published pricing, not live quotes. Off-ramp figures (Whitebird, Cifra Markets) are taken directly from each provider's own published fee page — but they're not live quotes either: neither provider has a public API for their tariffs, fees can change without notice, and we re-check these manually every so often. Bank transfer is shown only for contrast and is likewise based on typical, not live, pricing.

Questions
