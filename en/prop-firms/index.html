The prop-firm industry is not government-regulated. We only include firms with a publicly confirmed payout history and a trust rating. This is not a financial licence or government accreditation.

How we pick firms

There is no legal "prop-firm licence" in most countries, so instead of one "licensed" bar we use two tiers — things that never get softened, and things that can.

A hard no, no exceptions:

  • the firm (or an entity tied to it) is on an official regulator warning list or blacklist — e.g. the Bank of Russia, the SEC, the FCA;
  • the firm is under sanctions;
  • a confirmed, systematic pattern of non-payment complaints — not isolated reviews, a pattern;
  • documented retroactive rule changes against already-funded traders;
  • no verifiable legal registration anywhere.

If a firm clears that bar, we then look at reliability signals — these don't all have to be fully met; when one isn't, the firm is listed with a "Why it's listed" note:

  • a Trustpilot rating of 4.0+ with a meaningful number of reviews;
  • a confirmed multi-year payout history — or a newer firm (a year or two) with nothing else raising concerns on the hard criteria above;
  • ideally, backing by a regulated broker (e.g. FTMO ↔ OANDA, Blueberry Funded ↔ Blueberry Markets).

Some firms in the table do not clear every reliability signal (for example, Topstep's Trustpilot score is lower; BrightFunded and Blueberry Funded have fewer reviews). Those firms are listed with a "Why it's listed" note on their page — read it and judge the risk yourself.
