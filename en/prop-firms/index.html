The prop-firm industry is not government-regulated. We only include firms with a publicly confirmed payout history and a trust rating. This is not a financial licence or government accreditation.

How we pick firms

There is no legal "prop-firm licence" in most countries, so instead of "licensed" we look at signals of reliability:

  • a confirmed multi-year payout history — not just the firm's own claims;
  • a Trustpilot rating of 4.3+ with a large number of reviews;
  • no retroactive rule changes for already-funded traders;
  • ideally, backing by a regulated broker (e.g. FTMO ↔ OANDA, Blueberry Funded ↔ Blueberry Markets).

Some firms in the table do not clear every criterion (for example, Topstep's Trustpilot score is lower; BrightFunded and Blueberry Funded have fewer reviews). Those firms are listed with a "Why it's listed" note on their page — read it and judge the risk yourself.
