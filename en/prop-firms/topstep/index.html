Topstep: Trading Combine rules

One of the oldest futures prop firms (since 2012). Evaluation via a monthly-subscription Trading Combine.

Russian residents effectively can't trade with Topstep. Like most US futures prop firms, Topstep complies with OFAC restrictions — Russia is on its list of ineligible jurisdictions. Registration, a funded account, and payouts are closed to Russian residents regardless of a second passport or payment method. Confirm current status directly with Topstep support before paying for an evaluation.
