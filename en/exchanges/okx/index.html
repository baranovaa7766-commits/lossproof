OKX: Exchange Overview

One of the largest global exchanges (since 2017). Russian residents currently retain trading access, but pending Russian legislation could restrict platforms without a Russian license as early as summer 2026.

For Russian residents: under Law No. 282-FZ, from 1 July 2027 digital currency (crypto) transactions will be allowed only through digital currency circulation organisers.
