Compare Crypto Exchanges

Licenses and regulation, access for Russian residents, spot trading fees, and deposit methods — for vetted exchanges, in one table.

For Russian residents: under Law No. 282-FZ, from 1 July 2027 digital currency (crypto) transactions will be allowed only through digital currency circulation organisers. The exchanges and cash-out services on this site are foreign companies. This is reference material: once it is known who receives that status, we will update the routes. Details

We list only licences confirmed by regulator registers or the exchanges' official announcements. A MiCA licence is usually held by a European arm and doesn't cover the exchange's main site. That's not a safety guarantee or financial advice — crypto exchange regulation is far weaker than bank regulation, and access status for Russian residents is unstable at many platforms and can change without notice.

How we pick exchanges

The comparison covers large international exchanges most often mentioned by traders from Russia and the CIS. For each we show which licences are confirmed by official registers (ESMA for MiCA in the EU, FSA in Seychelles) and which aren't — MEXC, for example, has none in these registers.

We separately show what each exchange's user agreement says about Russian residents: "not restricted" or "restrictions apply", with details on the exchange's card. All exchanges are shown the same way, with no links to their sites: this is reference information, not a recommendation or an invitation to use an exchange.

WhiteBIT is not included in this comparison. The exchange has blocked users from Russia since 2022, and in January 2026 Russia's Prosecutor General officially designated WhiteBIT and parent company W Group an "undesirable organization" — taking part in such an organisation's activities is an administrative offence in Russia, and a repeat offence is criminal (Art. 20.33 of the Code of Administrative Offences, Art. 284.1 of the Criminal Code).

HTX (Huobi) is also excluded: on 23 July 2026 the EU added HTX (Huobi Global SA) to its 21st sanctions package against Russia — since 23 August 2026 EU persons may not carry out any transactions with the exchange.
