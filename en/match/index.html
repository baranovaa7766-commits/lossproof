Prop Firm Matcher

There are dozens of prop firms out there, and most comparison sites just list all of them. We compare only 6 vetted firms (see our selection criteria) and help you figure out which one actually fits you — by market, budget, payout method, and how much the firm's track record matters to you.

Why 6 firms, not 50

The prop firm industry isn't government-regulated — there's no such thing as a "prop firm license." Instead, we use two tiers of criteria: a hard no with no exceptions (official regulator warnings, sanctions, systematic non-payment complaints, retroactive rule changes, no legal registration) and reliability signals that don't all have to be fully met — a Trustpilot rating of 4.0+, a multi-year payout history, or a newer firm with nothing else raising concerns (see the "Compare prop firms" page for details). The matcher looks for the best fit only among firms that clear the hard bar, not the whole market — a deliberate trade-off: fewer options, but every one is already safe.

If nothing looks like a perfect fit, that's an honest result too. Check the full comparison with filters for every field, or read "Prop Firm Red Flags" to evaluate a firm that isn't on our list yourself.
