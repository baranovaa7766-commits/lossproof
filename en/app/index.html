The LossProof app

Personal finance tracking for everyone: spending, budget, savings and net worth in one place — on your phone and your computer. The app is in development. At launch it will be completely free: no subscriptions and no paid features.

What the app will do

  • Quick entry. Log spending or income in a couple of seconds, including as one phrase: "taxi 412 yesterday".
  • Budget. Category limits, rollover to the next month and a hint of how much you can spend today to stay within the month.
  • Bills and subscriptions. The app finds recurring charges by itself and reminds you about them in advance.
  • Goals and savings. A holiday, an emergency fund, a big purchase — with how much to set aside each month.
  • Net worth. Accounts, deposits, property, loans and debts — and how your net worth changes over time.
  • Forecast. How much money you'll have on your cards in 30–90 days, taking recurring payments into account.
  • Reports. Where your money goes — by category, month and day.
  • For the self-employed and sole traders. Revenue, expenses, profit and a tax estimate with deadline reminders.
  • Sections. Personal money, business and investments — separately or all together.
  • Phone and computer. The same data on all your devices.

What the app is not

It's a tool for tracking your own money. It doesn't give investment advice, doesn't recommend specific banks, exchanges, brokers or other services, and doesn't sell financial products.
