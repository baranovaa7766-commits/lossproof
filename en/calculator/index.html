Why the "amount" a prop firm approves isn't what lands in your account

Your prop firm sends your payout in USD (or EUR/GBP), usually via bank transfer, crypto, or a payout partner like RiseWorks. For traders in Russia and the CIS, the next step is usually buying USDT on an exchange and then converting it to rubles or a local currency through an off-ramp service — Wise and Revolut aren't an option. Your final amount depends on two stages at once: the spread/rate at each step, and the fees charged by both the exchange and the off-ramp. Those losses stack up fast, easily 3-5% — real money on a five-figure payout. This calculator exists to make that comparison transparent before you pick a route.
